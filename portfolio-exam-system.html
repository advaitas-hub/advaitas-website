Competitive Exam Question Paper System

A question-paper generation system built for a PU college's competitive mock tests — managing question banks, paper structuring, and randomized paper generation for exam cycles.

Client PU College
Duration 4 months
Category Software Development

The Challenge

The college needed an efficient way to generate competitive exam question papers for mock tests. The manual process was time-consuming, prone to errors, and made it difficult to ensure fair distribution of questions across difficulty levels and topics. They needed a system that could manage large question banks and generate randomized papers quickly.

What We Did

We're building a comprehensive question paper generation system that manages question banks, allows for paper structuring based on difficulty and topic distribution, and generates randomized papers for each exam cycle. The system ensures fairness while reducing the administrative burden on faculty.

Deliverables

Question Bank Management
Paper Template Configuration
Automated Paper Generation
Difficulty Level Balancing
Print-Ready Output
Historical Tracking
